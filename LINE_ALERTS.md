# LINE stock alerts

The `LINE stock alerts` workflow runs after each successful `Refresh Top 10 board (30 minutes)` run on `main`. Its automatic mode is `scheduled`: a quota-aware five-stock summary before or during the US session. GitHub scheduling can be delayed. It reads the published Top 10 board and rechecks the existing `ranking_policy.is_entry` checklist for regular-session entry labels. It never places orders or changes ranking scores.

Scheduled summaries include exactly five validated instruments and can contain watch stocks. Entry labels still require score at least 80 with full scoring coverage, quote in the entry zone, current reward/risk at least 2, the existing spread/fundamental checks, ranking no older than 35 minutes, quote no older than 15 minutes, and no future source timestamps. Missing checks do not count as passed. Manual `scan` retains the former new-entry-only behavior. Alerts cover the board's Top 10, drawn from the whole-catalog ranking; they do not claim a fresh quote scan of every listed security.

Each scheduled alert is a five-stock Thai briefing from ranked equities, with ETFs used only when needed and labelled explicitly. Fewer than five validated instruments blocks scheduled delivery. A watch stock is never labelled as a confirmed entry. A scheduled slot has its own durable deduplication key; the same stocks can reappear with updated prices and news. Manual `scan` instead deduplicates new signals per symbol and New York market date.

One LINE push contains a one-page PNG plus detailed Thai text. It shows business, Thai sector and industry labels, source quote time, score, situation versus entry zone, stop, target, current reward/risk, upside/downside percentages, numeric fundamentals, dated news links, and remaining entry checks. Daily EMA20/50/200 are calculated from the ranking's verified history; RSI14 uses that history's existing calculation. The daily-bar date and short/long trend interpretations are explicit, and these indicators never upgrade entry eligibility. Each of up to two relevant articles has its source date, evidence scope, potential positive and negative effects, and a decision-related reading guide. Reviewed source summaries are distinct from conditional headline interpretations and publisher opinions. A short glossary explains the terms and position sizing. Generic guarantee/disclaimer sentences are omitted; specific missing data and company risks remain visible. Monthly quota is checked before sending. Multiple message objects in one push count as one delivery to this one recipient.

News is fetched once per selected stock through the existing bounded Yahoo Finance adapter. A transport or response-format failure can use one bounded public Yahoo RSS request; authentication denials, rate limits and explicit bot blocks stop retrieval. RSS requests have a ten-second timeout, a one-MiB response cap, no authentication, and at most one validated redirect to a canonical RSS path on finance.yahoo.com or feeds.finance.yahoo.com within the original time budget. Company relevance and source timestamps are validated; requesting a ticker-specific feed alone does not establish company relevance. News within seven days is preferred; older context is labelled and expires after thirty days. Separately reviewed primary-source Thai summaries in `stock_alert_news.json` expire seven days after review. Provider headlines are displayed as attributed source context with topic-specific conditional implications, not invented verified positive/negative findings. Feed errors remain unknown, with sanitized diagnostic codes retained in the report metadata. News never silently overrides the entry policy. `Check stock briefing news feeds` tests the live feed without LINE credentials or message delivery.

Images and public report facts are published on `line-alert-media`, separate from application code. LINE receives immutable commit URLs; both PNGs must be publicly readable with the expected content type and exact bytes before the send is reserved. Rendering uses Pillow/RAQM and Noto Sans Thai. Runtime report files are retained as GitHub workflow artifacts for seven days. The media and state branches contain no recipient IDs, tokens, personal balances or private account data.

## Configuration

Repository Actions Secrets: `LINE_CHANNEL_ACCESS_TOKEN` and `LINE_USER_ID`. The built-in `GITHUB_TOKEN` writes delivery state and public report media to their separate branches. The sender verifies the token's bot basic ID against `@618mpszx` and checks that the configured recipient is accessible. Add the OA as a friend before the setup test. No webhook, friend-list read, personal chat access, broker login, or channel secret is needed.

Run manually with `mode=scheduled` for the currently due planned round, `mode=schedule-check` to read the live quota and monthly allocation without sending, `mode=test` for the one-time setup message, `mode=dry-run` to count current signals without sending, `mode=scan` for a new-entry-only alert, or `mode=preview` for the one-time requested format example. Preview reports use real timestamped market data and can contain watch stocks. A successful LINE API response means LINE accepted the request, not a read receipt.

## Delivery state and failures

The isolated `line-alert-state` branch stores HMAC identifiers and the pending public-market alert text. It never stores LINE IDs, tokens or account profiles. A durable reservation, exact message body and LINE retry UUID are saved before sending. Uncertain sends reuse that exact request within its expiry. Expired uncertain sends are suppressed for the same market date to avoid duplicate or stale notifications; the report exposes `expired_pending`. This intentionally favors avoiding stale/duplicate delivery over claiming guaranteed delivery.

Quota, permission, malformed-data and delivery failures are visible in Actions. Logs and summaries contain counts/status only, not credentials or recipient IDs. Tests use fake delivery and storage; they never call LINE. To pause alerts, disable `LINE stock alerts` in GitHub Actions; the dashboard/ranking jobs continue independently.

Official references: [LINE retry handling](https://developers.line.biz/en/docs/messaging-api/retrying-api-request/), [LINE Messaging API](https://developers.line.biz/en/reference/messaging-api/), [GitHub workflow events](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/events-that-trigger-workflows).

## Report review and frequency

`Review industry and analysis stock report` recalculates the verified daily snapshot and renders the report without LINE credentials, message delivery, media publication, or ranking publication. Review/preview modes can display independently timestamped regular close and extended-hours quotes; old closes remain watch-only and retain their source clock. Regular automated entries still require current regular-session quotes. The workflow does not call an LLM or consume ChatGPT tokens each time it runs.

The sender allocates the live finite LINE quota across actual NYSE sessions in each Thai calendar month. `pandas-market-calendars` accounts for holidays, early closes and US DST. Approximately 30% of full-day rounds are pre-market, between 07:15 and 09:15 New York time; regular rounds run from 09:45 to 15:45, ending earlier on early-close days. Candidate slots are 30 minutes apart. A full 300-unit month is normally 13–14 reports per full session, with four pre-market reports. Month-edge partial sessions are included in the allocation. Thai times move one hour later when New York switches to standard time.

One push to this one recipient consumes one quota unit, including the image and text objects. The quota endpoint includes other OA usage. Each day's plan is frozen when first checked, and the next day is allocated from the remaining budget and sessions. Charges are persisted before sending to cover the approximate consumption API's lag. Quota is checked again after report preparation. Monthly reset observations near the boundary and a new Thai month start refresh the ledger; live quota always bounds new sends. The account's plan and payment settings are never changed.

Starting near month-end uses the remaining trading slots; it does not send a backlog to exhaust unused quota. A delayed event can send only within the currently planned 15-minute window. Missed or expired rounds are not queued, and can be absorbed into later days when capacity permits. The existing half-hour pipeline supports at most 18 rounds on a full trading day; `schedule-check` explicitly reports any quota above the remaining capacity. The actual configured account limit was verified as 300 units on 30 September 2026.

Pre-market cards use only an independently timestamped, USD, PRE-session quote no older than 15 minutes. Missing PRE prices show the regular close as dated context. A price inside the zone with valid fundamentals and R:R can be labelled `pre_candidate`, never a regular-session confirmed entry. Saved company Bid/Ask values cannot confirm pre-market liquidity; the card asks the reader to check current Bid/Ask in Dime and use a limit price. The regular entry audit and readiness flags remain unchanged. Stops and targets retain the existing daily-chart plan basis.
